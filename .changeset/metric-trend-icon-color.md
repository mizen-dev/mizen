`MetricTrend` accepts `iconColor`: `"brand"` (the default), `"neutral"`, `"blue"`, `"purple"`, `"green"` or `"orange"`. It colors the icon tile by category with the `AvatarFallback` palette, so stat cards of different kinds can be told apart. Existing cards keep the brand tile.
