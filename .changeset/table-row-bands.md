A hovered or selected `Table` row is now a band with rounded ends. Selected rows that touch form one band, and the rules above and below a band give way to its edge. The hover band is lighter, so a secondary control or a neutral avatar keeps its shape inside it. A `grid` table keeps square bands. `DataTable` marks a selected record with the same band when row selection is on.
